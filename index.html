Article 5

Top 5 Common Mistakes That Lead to Govt Exam Form Rejection

  • Exceeding Maximum KB Limits: Uploading a 20.5 KB file when the limit is strictly 20.0 KB causes immediate portal error. ExactKBwala prevents this by providing exact output size.
  • Wrong Aspect Ratio / Distorted Face: Stretching images manually corrupts face recognition. ExactKBwala maintains proper aspect ratio.
  • Dark / Shadowy Background: Ensure light or white backgrounds for photo uploads.
  • Illegible Signature Ink: Sign using dark blue or black ink on plain white unruled paper.
