4. Pro Tips To Take Perfect Visa Photo With Phone

  • Use Daylight, Not Flash: Flash creates red-eye and white spots. Use natural window light.
  • Wear Dark Clothes: Don't wear white - it will blend into white background. Wear dark blue, black, or maroon.
  • Remove Glasses: Even if you wear glasses daily, you must remove them for visa photo.
  • Keep Hair Back: Full face, forehead, and both ears must be visible.
  • No Filter: No beauty mode, no Snapchat filter, no editing. Keep it 100% natural.
  • Neutral Face: Mouth closed, no smiling with teeth, no frowning.

5. How To Make Visa Photo For Kids & Babies?

For babies, lay your baby on a white sheet on a bed and take a photo from above. Eyes must be open. No pacifier, no hands, no toys. For toddlers, make them sit in a high chair in front of a white wall. Take 30 photos quickly - kids move a lot. You will get one perfect shot.

The tool will crop it to the exact size. For kids, the rules are the same - white background, both eyes open, neutral face.

6. 7 Mistakes That Get Your Visa Photo Rejected

  • 1. Using Selfie: #1 mistake. Ask someone else to take it.
  • 2. Wearing Glasses: Remove them, even if you always wear them.
  • 3. Smiling With Teeth: Keep mouth closed, neutral face.
  • 4. Wrong Size: USA is 2x2 inch, UK is 35x45mm. Don't mix them up!
  • 5. Dark Background: Must be plain white or light grey, not beige or dark.
  • 6. Shadow On Face: Don't stand too close to wall. Stand 1 foot away.
  • 7. Old Photo: Must be taken within last 6 months. If you changed hairstyle or beard, take a new one.
