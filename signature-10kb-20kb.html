💡 3. Signature Karte Waqt Ye 8 Baatein Dhyaan Rakho

  • 2. CAPITAL Me Sign Mat Karo: Poora naam CAPITAL LETTER me likh ke sign mat karo. Apna asli wala sign karo jo tum roz karte ho.
  • 3. Sign Ko Kaat-Peet Mat Karo: Sign karte waqt agar galat ho jaye to usi paper par kaat ke dobara mat karo. Naya paper lo aur naya sign karo.
  • 4. Photo Me Chhaya Na Aaye: Sign ki photo khinchne waqt tumhare haath ya mobile ki chhaya sign par nahi aani chahiye. Nahi to sign kaala dikhega.
  • 5. Sign Ko Crop Kar Lo: Photo khinchne ke baad Gallery me jaake usko crop kar lo, taaki sirf sign dikhe, extra white paper na dikhe.
  • 6. JPG Me Hi Rakho: Signature hamesha JPG me hi rakho. PNG ya PDF me mat bhejo.
  • 7. Size Check Kar Lo: Download ke baad Details me dekho - 18KB, 19KB, 20KB sab chalega.
  • 8. Sign Ka Print Bhi Rakh Lo: Form wale sign ki ek photo apne paas sambhal ke rakho, taaki Exam me wahi sign kar sako.
