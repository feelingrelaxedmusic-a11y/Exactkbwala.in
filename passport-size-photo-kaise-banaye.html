💡 3. Passport Photo Khinchwate Waqt Ye 10 Baatein Dhyaan Rakho

  • 1. Chehra Seedha Rakho: Chehra bilkul seedha rakho, daaye-baaye mat dekho. Camera me dekho.
  • 2. Dono Kaan Dikhe: Baal se kaan mat chhupao. Dono kaan saaf dikhne chahiye.
  • 3. Chashma Mat Pehno: Passport photo me chashma nahi pehna jata. Bina chashme ke photo khinchao.
  • 4. Topi / Mask Mat Pehno: Topi, gamchha, mask, ya chashma - kuch bhi mat pehno.
  • 5. Halki Smile Karo: Bahut zyada daant dikha ke mat haso. Halki si smile karo, ya bina smile ke seedhi photo khinchao.
  • 6. Kapde Ka Rang: White kapde mat pehno, kyunki background bhi white hai. Koi dark rang ka kapda pehno - jaise neela, laal, ya hara.
  • 7. Baal Sahi Rakho: Baal se chehra mat chhupao. Ladkiyan baal ko peeche karke photo khinchwayein.
  • 8. Photo Abhi Ki Ho: 6 mahine se purani photo mat lagao. Abhi ki, haal ki photo lagao.
  • 9. Photo Saaf Ho: Photo dhundhli nahi honi chahiye. Agar photo dhundhli aayi to dobara khincho.
  • 10. Ek Se Zyada Photo Khincho: Ek hi baar me 5-6 photo khinch lo, fir unme se sabse achhi wali chun lo.
