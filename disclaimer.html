2. Photo Resizer Tool Ke Baare Me Disclaimer

Hamara Photo Resizer Tool aapki photo ko aapke bataye gaye size me karne ki poori koshish karta hai, jaise 20KB, 50KB, 100KB, 200KB. Lekin kuch baatein aapko samajhni chahiye:

  • Quality Ka Fark: Jab photo ko bahut zyada chhota kiya jaata hai, to uski quality thodi kam ho sakti hai. Ye har tool me hota hai. Agar aapki photo pehle se hi bahut dhundhli hai, to resize ke baad wo aur dhundhli lag sakti hai.
  • Exact Size: Hum 99% koshish karte hai ki size bilkul exact aaye. Lekin kabhi-kabhi 1KB ya 2KB ka fark aa sakta hai, kyunki har photo ka format alag hota hai. Form bharne se pehle ek baar photo ki properties me jaake size zaroor check kar lein.
  • Photo Ka Format: Hamara tool JPG, JPEG aur PNG format ko support karta hai. Agar aapki photo kisi aur format me hai, to pehle usko JPG me badal lein.
  • Aapki Zimmedari: Photo upload karne se pehle ye dekh lein ki photo saaf hai, background sahi hai, aur chehra saaf dikh raha hai. Galat photo ko resize karne se wo sahi nahi ho jayegi.

💡 Ek Zaroori Salah: Form ka photo hamesha halki background wali, saamne se khinchi hui, aur haal ki photo honi chahiye. Purani ya tope wali photo ka istemal na karein. Sarkari form me galat photo lagane se form reject ho sakta hai.
