❓ ExactKBwala Kya Hai?

ExactKBwala.in ek 100% Free Image Compressor Tool hai. Ye ek aisi website hai jo aapke photo aur signature ko bilkul exact KB me badal deti hai.

Jaise aapko form me chahiye 20KB ka photo, to ye tool aapko 19KB ya 21KB nahi dega, bilkul 20KB hi dega. Isi liye iska naam ExactKBwala hai - matlab Exact KB Wala.

Ye tool specially un forms ke liye banaya gaya hai jisme photo ka size fix hota hai - jaise SSC, UPSC, IBPS, Railway, UP Police, UPSSSC, aur bhi bahut saare govt forms.

😔 Ye Tool Maine Kyu Banaya? - Meri Asli Kahani

Ye kahani 100% sach hai, isliye dhyaan se padhna.

Jab maine pehli baar SSC GD ka form bhara tha, to mujhe photo upload karne me hi 2 din lag gaye. Form me likha tha - Photo 20KB se 50KB ke beech me hona chahiye. Mera photo tha 2MB ka. Maine usko chhota karne ki bahut koshish ki.

Pehle maine YouTube dekha, phir ek app download ki. App ne photo ko 15KB kar diya, par mera chehra itna blur ho gaya ki pehchana hi nahi ja raha tha. Phir maine dusri app try ki, usne 80KB kar diya. Form phir se reject.

Ant me main cyber cafe gaya. Us bhaiya ne 50 rupaye liye, aur 2 minute me photo bana diya. Mujhe laga kaam ho gaya. Par jab maine form submit kiya, to phir se error aa gaya - “Photo size does not match”.

Us din mujhe bahut gussa aaya, aur rona bhi aaya. Mere 50 rupaye bhi gaye, aur form bhi nahi bhara gaya. Ghar aake maine socha - “Yaar, agar mere saath aisa ho raha hai, to pure India me lakhon bachon ke saath aisa hota hoga. Koi aisa tool kyu nahi hai jo bilkul free ho, sahi size de, aur photo ko kharab bhi na kare?”

⚙️ Ye Tool Kaise Kaam Karta Hai? - Step By Step

Isko chalana bahut hi aasan hai, koi bhi chala sakta hai, chahe usko mobile chalana bhi thoda-thoda hi aata ho:

  1. Step 1 - Photo Chuno: Sabse pehle apna photo select karo. Ye photo aapke gallery se aayega.
  2. Step 2 - Size Chuno: Aapko kitne KB ka chahiye? 20KB, 50KB, 100KB - jo form me likha hai, wahi select karo.
  3. Step 3 - Compress Dabao: Compress button dabate hi tool kaam shuru kar deta hai.
  4. Step 4 - Download Karo: 2-3 second me aapko exact size wala photo mil jayega. Usse download karke seedha form me laga do.
