💡 3. Photo 20KB Karte Waqt In 7 Baaton Ka Dhyaan Rakho

  • 1. Original Photo Use Karo: WhatsApp se aayi hui photo ya uska screenshot mat use karo. Wo photo pehle se hi kharab ho jaati hai. Hamesha camera ki original photo use karo.
  • 2. Background White Rakho: Photo ke peeche ka background white ya halka hona chahiye. Lal, neela ya design wali deewar ke saamne photo mat khincho.
  • 3. Light Achhi Rakho: Photo din ki roshni me khincho. Andhere me khinchi hui photo kaali aayegi aur form me reject ho sakti hai.
  • 4. Chehra Seedha Rakho: Chehra ekdum seedha camera ki taraf hona chahiye. Tedha-meda muh mat banao.
  • 5. Size Dobara Check Karo: Photo download karne ke baad Gallery me jaake uski Details me dekho. Wahan 19KB, 20KB, 21KB likha hona chahiye. Ye sab chalega.
  • 6. JPG Me Hi Rakho: SSC sirf JPG / JPEG leta hai. PNG ya PDF me photo mat bhejo.
  • 7. Ek Se Zyada Photo Bana Ke Rakho: 20KB, 50KB aur 100KB - teeno size ki photo bana ke ek folder me rakh lo. Kabhi bhi kisi form me kaam aa jayegi.
