1. What Is The Correct Visa Photo Size?

For most countries, especially the USA, the standard visa photo size is 2 x 2 inches. This is the same as a US passport photo. It is a perfect square. Many people confuse it with a passport photo from other countries, like 35mm x 45mm in the UK or Europe. But for a US visa, you must use 2x2 inch.

If you are applying for a Schengen visa, Canada visa, or UK visa, the size is slightly different. So always check your specific visa form. But in this guide, we are focusing on the most popular one - the US visa photo - because that is what most people search for.

2. Visa Photo Background - White Is A Must

Your visa photo background must be plain white. Not off-white, not light gray, not blue. Just plain white. The embassy uses facial recognition software, and a white background helps the software read your face clearly.

Do not take your photo in front of a door, a bookshelf, or a colorful wall. Find a plain white wall in your home. If you do not have one, hang a white bedsheet behind you. Make sure there are no wrinkles, shadows, or folds in the sheet. The background should look clean and professional, like a studio photo.

3. Face, Expression & Head Position - The Golden Rules

Your face is the most important part. Follow these rules:

  • Look Straight: Look directly at the camera. Do not look sideways or tilt your head.
  • Neutral Expression: Keep your face relaxed. A tiny closed-mouth smile is okay, but do not show teeth. No laughing, no frowning.
  • Eyes Open: Both eyes must be open and clearly visible. No red-eye, no squinting.
  • Centered: Your head must be in the center of the photo. Leave equal space on both sides.
  • Full Face: Your full face from top of hair to bottom of chin must show. Do not let hair cover your forehead or eyes.

Your head height should be 1 inch to 1-3/8 inch from chin to top of head. If your head is too small, the photo looks far away. If it is too big, your chin or hair will be cut off. Both will get rejected.

4. Glasses, Clothes & Accessories - What To Wear?

Here is a rule many people miss: Remove your glasses for your visa photo. Even if you wear glasses every single day, you must take them off for the photo. Glasses cause glare and hide your eyes. The only exception is if you have a medical reason and you have a signed doctor's note, but even then it is risky. So just take them off.

For clothes, wear your normal daily clothes. Do not wear a uniform, a white t-shirt, or camouflage. A white shirt will blend into the white background and make you look like a floating head. Wear a dark blue, black, maroon, or any colorful top. It will create a nice contrast and make you look sharp.

Hats, caps, and headbands for fashion are not allowed. Head coverings are only allowed for religious reasons, and your full face must still be visible. No shadow from the covering should fall on your face.

5. Lighting & Quality - Make It Look Professional

Good lighting is the secret to a professional-looking photo. The best light is soft, natural daylight. Stand near a big window in the morning. Do not stand in direct harsh sunlight because it creates dark shadows on one side of your face.

Do not use flash. Flash creates a white spot on your forehead and red-eye. Your photo must be sharp, clear, and in focus. No blurry, no grainy, no dark photo. And never use a filter, beauty mode, or Photoshop to change your face. The embassy wants to see the real you, not a filtered version.

6. How To Make Visa Photo From Phone? Step-by-Step

You do not need to pay $17 at CVS or Walgreens. You can make it at home in 5 minutes:

Step 1: Find Light & Wall. Find a white wall near a window. Morning light is best.

Step 2: Set Up Phone. Put your phone on a table 5 feet away. Use the back camera. Ask a friend or family member to take it. Selfies are not allowed because they distort your face.

Step 3: Take 5-6 Photos. Stand straight, look at camera, keep shoulders visible. Pick the best one.

Step 4: Crop To 2x2. Crop it to a perfect square, 600x600 pixels. Keep your head centered.

Step 5: Compress & Check KB. For DS-160, keep file under 240KB in JPG. Use a free tool to compress.

7. Visa Photo For Kids, Babies & Family

If you are applying for a family visa, your kids also need a photo with the same rules. For babies, lay them on a white sheet on a bed and take a photo from above. Their eyes must be open. No pacifier, no hands, no toys in the photo. For toddlers, make them sit on a chair in front of a white wall. Take many photos fast because kids move a lot. It is hard, but keep trying - you will get one perfect shot.

8. 7 Mistakes That Get Your Visa Photo Rejected

  • 1. Wearing Glasses: The #1 reason for rejection. Remove them.
  • 2. Smiling With Teeth: Keep mouth closed.
  • 3. Wrong Background: Must be plain white, not blue or gray.
  • 4. Using Selfie: Selfies distort your face. Ask someone else.
  • 5. Old Photo: If you grew a beard or changed hairstyle, take a new one.
  • 6. Using Filter: No beauty filter, no editing.
  • 7. Wrong Size: Must be 2x2 inch square, not rectangle.

9. Visa Photo vs Passport Photo - Are They Same?

Yes! For the USA, the visa photo and passport photo are exactly the same - 2x2 inch, white background, same rules. So if you already have a good passport photo taken in the last 6 months, you can use the same photo for your visa. It will save you time and money. Just make sure it is still recent and you look the same.

10. Final Checklist Before You Submit

  • Is it 2x2 inch and square?
  • Is background plain white?
  • Are both eyes open?
  • Did you remove glasses?
  • Is head centered and correct size?
  • Is photo less than 6 months old?
  • Is file JPG and under 240KB?

If YES to all, your photo is ready!
