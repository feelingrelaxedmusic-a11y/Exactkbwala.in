9. 5 Mistakes To Avoid When Converting JPG To PDF

  • 1. Blurry Photos: If your original JPG is blurry, your PDF will also be blurry. Always take clear, sharp photos in good light.
  • 2. Wrong Order: Always check the order of pages before converting. Page 1 should be first.
  • 3. Wrong Page Size: For USA documents, use Letter size. For other countries, use A4. Wrong size can cut off your photo.
  • 4. Using Low-Quality Screenshots: Use original photos, not screenshots of photos.
  • 5. Using Unsafe Websites: Never upload your ID or personal documents to unknown websites that save your files. Use a trusted tool that deletes files automatically.
