Why Govt Forms Ask for 20KB Photo?

Government websites like SSC, UPSC, IBPS, and Railway have lakhs of students applying. If everyone uploads a 2MB photo, their server will become slow. That's why they ask for a small size photo - usually 20KB to 50KB.

It is not your fault. Your mobile camera takes a 2MB to 5MB photo by default. You just need to compress it to the right size.
