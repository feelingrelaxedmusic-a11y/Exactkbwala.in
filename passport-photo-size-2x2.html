1. What Is The Correct Passport Photo Size?

The official and standard passport photo size in the United States is 2 x 2 inches. This size is used for US Passport, US Visa, Green Card, and most other federal applications. If you are applying for a passport in the USA, this is the size you must use. Do not use a 4x6 size or a passport size from another country, because it will be rejected.

Many people get confused between different sizes. For example, in India, passport photo size is 2x2 inch as well, but in the UK it is 35mm x 45mm. So always check your country first. For the USA, remember one simple rule: Square Photo, 2 by 2 Inch.

2. Passport Photo Background - The Most Important Rule

Your passport photo background must be plain white or off-white. No patterns, no trees, no walls with texture, no other people in the background. The background should be completely clean and evenly lit.

Why is this so strict? Because the government uses facial recognition software to verify your identity. If your background is dark, busy, or has shadows, the software cannot read your face properly, and your application gets rejected. So always use a white wall with good daylight.

If you do not have a white wall at home, you can stand in front of a white bedsheet. Just make sure there are no wrinkles or shadows on it.

3. Face, Expression, And Head Position Rules

This is where most people make mistakes. Your face is the main part of the photo, so follow these rules carefully:

  • Look Directly: Look straight at the camera. Do not look up, down, or sideways.
  • Neutral Expression: Keep a natural, neutral expression. A small, closed-mouth smile is okay, but do not show teeth or laugh.
  • Both Eyes Open: Both eyes must be clearly open and visible. No squinting.
  • Head Centered: Your head must be in the center of the photo. Not too high, not too low.
  • Full Face Visible: Your full face, from top of hair to bottom of chin, must be visible. Do not cover your face with hair.
  • No Head Tilt: Keep your head straight. Do not tilt left or right.

Your head height from the bottom of your chin to the top of your head should be between 1 inch and 1-3/8 inches. If your head is too small or too big in the frame, it will be rejected.

4. Glasses, Clothing, And Accessories - What Is Allowed?

Since 2016, the US State Department has a strict rule: You must remove your glasses for your passport photo, even if you wear them every day. This is because glasses create glare and hide your eyes. So take them off, even if you feel strange without them.

For clothing, wear your normal everyday clothes. Do not wear a uniform, camouflage, or a white shirt, because a white shirt will blend into the white background. A dark blue, black, or colorful top works best because it creates a nice contrast.

What about hats and head coverings? You cannot wear a hat, cap, or headband just for fashion. Head coverings are only allowed for religious or medical reasons, and even then, your full face must be visible. No shadows on your face from the head covering.

Jewelry is allowed, but keep it simple. Big earrings or necklaces that distract from your face are not a good idea. The focus should be on your face, not your accessories.

5. Lighting And Photo Quality - How To Get It Right

Good lighting can make or break your passport photo. The best light is natural daylight. Stand near a large window or go outside in open shade. Do not use direct harsh sunlight, because it creates hard shadows on your face.

Do not use flash. Flash creates red-eye and a bright white spot on your forehead. If your photo is too dark, too bright, blurry, or grainy, it will be rejected. Your photo must be sharp, clear, and in focus.

Also, do not use any filters, beauty mode, or editing apps. No smoothing your skin, no whitening your teeth, no changing your face shape. The photo must look like the real you.

6. How To Make Passport Size Photo From Phone At Home? (Step-by-Step)

You do not need to pay $15-$20 at a studio anymore. You can make a perfect passport photo from your phone in just 5 minutes. Follow this easy method:

Step 1: Find Good Light. Go to a room with a plain white wall and a big window. Morning light is best.

Step 2: Set Up Your Phone. Put your phone on a table or tripod, about 5 feet away from you. Use the back camera, not the selfie camera, for better quality. Ask a family member or friend to take the photo for you. Selfies are not allowed because they distort your face.

Step 3: Take The Photo. Stand straight, look at the camera, keep your shoulders visible, and take 5-6 photos. Choose the best one where your face looks natural and there is no shadow.

Step 4: Crop To 2x2 Inch. Open the photo and crop it to a square shape, 600 x 600 pixels. Keep your head in the center and leave some space above your hair and below your chin.

Step 5: Compress To Correct KB. For online forms, compress your photo to 50KB to 200KB in JPG format. If the file is too big, the form will not upload it.

7. Passport Photo Size For Kids And Babies

Taking a passport photo of a baby or a small child is tricky, but the rules are the same: 2x2 inch, white background, both eyes open. For babies under 1 year, you can lay the baby on a white sheet on the floor and take a photo from above. The baby's eyes must be open. No hands, toys, pacifier, or other people should be in the photo. For toddlers, you can make them sit on a high chair in front of a white wall. Take many photos quickly, because babies move a lot!

8. 7 Common Mistakes That Get Your Photo Rejected

  • 1. Using a Selfie: Selfies make your face look bigger on one side. Always ask someone else to take it.
  • 2. Wearing Glasses: Even if you always wear glasses, remove them for the photo.
  • 3. Smiling With Teeth: Keep your mouth closed. A big smile will get you rejected.
  • 4. Dark Background: Always use white. A beige or gray wall will not work.
  • 5. Using Old Photo: If your hair, beard, or face has changed a lot, take a new photo.
  • 6. Using Filter: No Snapchat filters, no beauty mode. Keep it 100% natural.
  • 7. Wrong Size: Do not upload a rectangular photo. It must be a perfect square, 2x2 inch.

9. Passport Photo Size For Visa, Green Card, And Other Uses

The good news is, once you have one perfect 2x2 inch photo, you can use it for many things. The US Visa photo size is also 2x2 inch. The Green Card Lottery (DV Lottery) photo is also 2x2 inch, but it must be 600x600 pixels in JPG format. Many job applications, school IDs, and driving license applications in the USA also accept the same 2x2 inch photo. So take one good photo and save it safely on your phone and computer. It will save you a lot of time and money.

10. Final Checklist Before You Submit

Before you upload or print your photo, check this list one by one:

  • Is it 2x2 inch and square?
  • Is the background plain white?
  • Are both eyes open and looking at the camera?
  • Did you remove your glasses?
  • Is your face in the center?
  • Is the photo less than 6 months old?
  • Is the file in JPG format and correct KB size?

If you said YES to all of these, congratulations! Your photo is ready to be approved.
