4. How To Take Perfect Photo With Your Phone? Pro Tips

  • Use Daylight, Not Flash: Flash creates red-eye and white spots. Use natural window light.
  • Don't Use Selfie: Selfies distort your face - one side looks bigger. Always ask someone else to take it.
  • Wear Dark Clothes: Don't wear white - it will blend into the white background. Wear dark blue, black, or maroon.
  • Remove Glasses: Even if you wear glasses daily, you must remove them for the photo.
  • Keep Hair Back: Your full face, forehead, and both ears should be visible. Tie your hair back.
  • No Filter: No beauty mode, no Snapchat filter, no editing. Keep it 100% natural.

5. How To Make Passport Photo For Kids & Babies Online?

Making a passport photo for a baby is tricky, but you can do it at home too. Lay your baby on a white sheet on a bed and take a photo from above. The baby's eyes must be open. No pacifier, no hands, no toys in the photo. For toddlers, make them sit in a high chair in front of a white wall. Take 20-30 photos quickly because kids move a lot - you will get one perfect shot.

For kids, the rules are the same - 2x2 inch, white background, both eyes open. The online tool will crop it perfectly.

7. 7 Mistakes That Get Your Home Passport Photo Rejected

  • 1. Using Selfie: The #1 mistake. Ask someone else to take it.

  • 3. Smiling With Teeth: Keep mouth closed, neutral face.
  • 4. Dark Background: Must be plain white, not beige or gray.

  • 7. Wrong Size: Must be 2x2 inch square, not rectangle.
